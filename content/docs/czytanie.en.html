Privacy policy

Effective from: 13 September 2026  ·  Last updated: 21 September 2026

The app "Nauka czytania sylabami" (Reading by Syllables; on the home screen it appears as "Czytaj sylabami") helps children aged 4 to 8 learn to read Polish: it splits text into syllables and colours them alternately. It is meant to be used under the supervision of a parent or carer. The app has no accounts — there is no registration, no sign-in and no profile — and it collects no data that directly identifies you or your child (no names, email addresses, phone numbers or location). It processes only the technical identifiers and usage statistics described in section 2.

You can send a request about your data to either address. In line with Article 26 of the GDPR, we inform you that we carry out our data protection duties jointly: whichever of us receives a request first answers it, and we handle breach reports together.

3. The library of texts and your data

The app downloads the fairy tales, poems and stories from our content server (Supabase). It creates no account and no user session in the process — no identifier is created that could be traced back to you or your child. The app connects to the server with a public key that is the same for every installation, and asks only for the published texts and their pages.

The traffic goes one way: the app reads from the server and writes nothing to it. Reading progress, the list of texts already read, your own text and all the settings stay on the device and never reach us. As with any internet connection, the server sees the device's IP address and records it in the hosting provider's technical logs; we do not combine those logs with anything else and we build no profile from them.

What we do not collect: first or last name, email address, password, phone number, location data, contact list, photos, audio or video recordings. The app never asks for this data: it does not open the camera, the photo library or the microphone, and it never asks for permission to use them. On Android it declares a single permission: internet access.

We also do not collect the Advertising ID. That permission is explicitly removed from the app.

On iOS the app collects no IDFA (advertising identifier) and no IDFV (vendor identifier), and it does not track you or your child outside the app — it has nothing to track, so it does not use App Tracking Transparency.

The text you type or paste as your own never leaves the device. All that reaches our statistics is whether it was short, medium or long — never the text itself, not even an excerpt.

4. Protecting children

  • The app shows no ads. None, in any form.
  • We use no personalised advertising and no behavioural profiling.
  • We do not link data from this app with data from other apps or services.
  • We do not pass data to third parties for marketing purposes and we do not sell it.
  • The app creates no accounts, for children or for anyone else, so it asks a child for nothing.
  • Every purchase must be confirmed by an adult: before payment the app sets an addition task that a child still learning to read cannot solve. Three wrong answers in a row block purchases for ten minutes.

We act in accordance with the US COPPA act, Article 8 of the GDPR, the Google Play Families programme policies and, in the iOS version, Apple's App Store guidelines for apps in the Kids category.

6. Printing and PDF export

The document for printing is produced on the device and handed straight to the system print dialog. It does not pass through our server and we never store it. This applies both to texts from the library and to your own text.

While preparing the document, the app downloads the typeface from the Google Fonts server; all that goes there is a request for a font file, never the content of the document. If a publication has graphical page backgrounds, the app downloads them from our content server. Our statistics record only that an export happened, and whether it came from the library or from your own text.

7. In-app purchases

  • The full version is unlocked by a one-time purchase called "Dożywotnia Licencja" (Lifetime Licence). There is no subscription and no automatic renewal.
  • We do not store payment card or bank details. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play (Android) or Apple (iOS).
  • The purchase is tied only to an anonymous identifier drawn at random on the device — we do not know, and cannot find out, who made it.
  • You can restore the purchase with the "Restore purchases" button on the purchase screen and in the settings. On Android it is tied to your Google Play account, on iOS to your Apple Account, and Family Sharing is turned off.
  • Refunds on Android are handled by Google Play under its own terms; on iOS, by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com).

8. How long we keep data

  • Reading progress, settings and your own text: until the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared. They disappear together with the app.
  • Statistical data: kept by Firebase Analytics according to the project settings, for a maximum of 14 months.
  • Transaction data: for the period required by tax and accounting regulations.

9. Your rights

You have the right to request access to your data, its rectification, erasure or restriction of processing, to object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority: the authority for your place of residence, or the Polish authority responsible for us, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, UODO), under Article 77 of the GDPR.

Because the app has no accounts, we cannot link statistical data to a specific person or device. The most effective way to remove data from the device is therefore to clear the app's data or uninstall it. Details are in the Deleting data section.

About the app

The app splits every text into syllables and colours them alternately. Your child's eye stops getting lost mid-word, and reading turns from guessing into assembling.

Ages 4–8Polish only No adsNo account Android and iOS

How it works

  • Text is split automatically by Polish syllable rules and neighbouring syllables get different colours, so the child sees where one ends and the next begins.
  • You choose the way of splitting in the settings: phonetic (the way a word is spoken) or orthographic (the way a word is hyphenated at the end of a line).
  • Syllable colours, font size and background theme are set to whatever your child reads most comfortably.
  • The app remembers the page your child reached in each text and shows how much has been read.

What is inside

  • A library of texts: fairy tales, poems and stories ready to read, including classics of Polish children's literature.
  • Your own text: type or paste anything and see it in coloured syllables.
  • Print and PDF: export any text and print it to practise away from the screen.

The app works in Polish only — the syllable splitting is built on the rules of the Polish language.

What the app does not have

  • No accounts, no registration, no sign-in — you install it and start reading.
  • No ads and no promotional content aimed at children.
  • No leaderboards, no countdown and no comparing with other children.
  • No access to the camera, photos, microphone, location or contacts.

Free version and full version

The free version lets you read the first texts, so you can check whether the app suits your child. Full access — the whole library and your own texts — is unlocked by the one-time "Lifetime Licence" purchase, with no subscription. Before payment the app sets an addition task, so a child cannot buy access on their own.

For teachers and therapists

The app needs no account and no sign-in, so there is no need to collect consent for creating children's accounts. It shows no ads and does not distinguish between several pupils on one device — progress is saved per device. Downloading new texts requires an internet connection. If you need a larger number of licences for your institution, write to us.

Authors

The app is made by Sebastian Rosiński and Dawid Adamczyk. It is part of the Kidalu series, which also includes Letters and Numbers.

Deleting data

The app has no user accounts, so there is no profile to close — and nothing on our side to delete. Everything the app remembers about you and your child is stored on the device.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of the data: reading progress, settings (syllable colours, font size, background theme, way of splitting syllables) and your own texts. No copy remains on our side, because the data never reached us.

If you came here looking for instructions on deleting an account: the app never creates one. It never asks for an email address or a password and it builds no profile of your child, so there is no account for us to delete.

How to clear data without uninstalling

Android:

  1. Open the Android Settings.
  2. Go to Apps and choose Czytaj sylabami.
  3. Open Storage and choose Clear data.

The app returns to its initial state. The full-version purchase is not lost: you can restore it with the "Restore purchases" button in the app's settings, because it is tied to your Google Play account rather than to the data in the app.

iPhone and iPad: iOS has no separate "clear app data" option. The only way is to delete the app:

  1. Open Settings → General → iPhone Storage (or iPad Storage).
  2. Choose Czytaj sylabami and tap Delete App.
  3. Or press and hold the app icon on the Home Screen and choose Delete App.

After reinstalling, restore the full-version purchase with the "Restore purchases" button in the settings, because it is tied to your Apple Account rather than to the data in the app.

Statistical data

Feature usage statistics are collected under an app instance identifier that points to no person. The identifier ceases to exist when the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared, and events collected before that are deleted automatically after 14 months at most.

If you want us to delete statistical data sooner, write to us. We will need the approximate period of use and the device model in order to find the right events.

Texts on our server

The server the app downloads fairy tales and poems from holds none of your data — the app only reads from it and writes nothing to it. There is no reading progress there, no texts of your own and no settings.

Purchase data

Purchase history is kept by Google Play, the App Store and RevenueCat. We cannot delete it, because it is the basis of your right to the full version of the app and an accounting record. Matters concerning payments and refunds are handled by Google Play (Android) or Apple (iOS).
