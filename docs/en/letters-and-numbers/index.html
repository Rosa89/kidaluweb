Letters and Numbers

The child traces the shape while the app watches the order and direction of every stroke. You pick the country at the start — it sets the alphabet, the handwriting style and the animals to collect.

  • Children aged 4–7
  • Poland, Germany, Austria
  • Works offline
  • No account, no ads

Get it on Google Play

What's inside

  • Tracing, not drawing

    The child follows the model rather than drawing freely — the app checks whether the hand stays on the line.

  • Fixed stroke order

    Every character has a defined stroke order and direction, matching the school handwriting of the chosen country. The app never contradicts the exercise book.

  • Poland, Germany, Austria

    For Poland, 35 upper and 35 lower case letters including ą, ć, ę, ł, ń, ó, ś, ź, ż. For Germany and Austria, the alphabet with ä, ö, ü, ß. Digits 0 to 9 everywhere.

  • Handwriting style and difficulty

    Six school scripts to choose from in Germany, two each in Poland and Austria. The tolerated deviation from the trace is set in the parent panel.

  • No time pressure

    No timer, no attempt limit. Accuracy counts, not speed.

  • Works offline

    All you need is a touchscreen. No account, no sign-in.

Who it's for

  • Children aged 4–7 getting ready to write
  • Parents who want to practise at home
  • Nurseries, reception classes and after-school clubs — including therapy work

Privacy

  • No ads or promotional content aimed at children
  • The app needs no account and no sign-in
  • Learning progress stays in the device's memory only

Compliant with COPPA, GDPR art. 8 and the Google Play Families policy.
