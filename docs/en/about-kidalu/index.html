What is Kidalu

Kidalu is a Polish studio of two dads and developers building educational apps for children aged 4–8. We teach through play and collect no data about children.

Who we are

Kidalu is made by Sebastian Rosiński and Dawid Adamczyk. It started with a question: how to help our own kids learn to read and write without a screen full of ads. The answer became apps we would want to install ourselves: simple, warm and safe.

What we make

We build Android apps that lead a child into the world of letters, syllables and numbers step by step, and both now come to iPhone and iPad too. Reading by Syllables, available in Polish only, teaches joining syllables into words, and Letters and Numbers practises writing with a finger on the screen. Both apps are on Google Play under the publisher name Kidalu, and now on the App Store too.

Why you can trust us

Kidalu apps contain no ads, build no behavioural profiles and do not track children outside the app. They comply with COPPA, GDPR art. 8 and the Google Play Families policies, and, on iOS, our apps also follow Apple's App Store guidelines for apps in the Kids category. We publish every document on this site and answer every question about data.

Our principles

  • No ads and no promotional content aimed at children
  • No profiling and no linking of data between apps
  • Learning at the child's pace, with no pressure and no leaderboards
  • Clear documents and quick answers to parents' questions
