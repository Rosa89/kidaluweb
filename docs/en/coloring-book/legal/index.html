Privacy policy

Effective from: 6 October 2026  ·  Last updated: 7 October 2026

“Coloring Book for Kids – Kidalu” (shown under the icon as: Coloring) is a colouring app for children aged 3–8. It is meant to be used under the supervision of a parent or carer. The app is completely free, has no purchases, needs no account and does not collect data that directly identifies you or your child (name, email address, phone number, precise location). The only data processed are the technical identifiers and usage statistics described in section 2.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The joint data controllers, with an equal share in the project, are:

  • Sebastian Rosiński — sebastian.rosinski.1989@gmail.com
  • Dawid Adamczyk — phantom0492@gmail.com

You can send a request about your data to either address. In line with Article 26 of the GDPR, we inform you that we carry out our data protection duties jointly: whichever of us receives a request first answers it, and we handle breach reports together.

The Firebase instance identifier is not linked to any person — it is assigned to the app on the device and changes when the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared. Your child’s drawings never leave the device — we do not send them to any server.

What we do not collect: name, email address, phone number, precise (GPS) location, contacts, photos from the gallery, audio or video recordings. The app does not ask for this data and has no system permissions to access it.

We also do not collect the Advertising ID — the permission for it has been removed from the app.

On iOS, the app does not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA) or the identifier for vendors (IDFV), and does not track you or your child outside the app — it does not use App Tracking Transparency, because there is nothing for it to cover.

3. Protecting children

  • The app shows no ads — none, in any form.
  • The app has no in-app purchases or subscriptions — all coloring pages are free.
  • We do not use personalised advertising or behavioural profiling.
  • We do not combine data from this app with data from other apps or services.
  • We do not share data with third parties for marketing purposes and we do not sell it.
  • Links that lead outside the app (the kidalu.com website, the privacy policy) are available to adults only, in the PIN-protected Parent zone.
  • We store the PIN only in hashed form (never as plain text).

We comply with the US Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), Article 8 of the GDPR, the Google Play Families policy and, on iOS, Apple’s App Store guidelines for apps in the Kids category.

5. Speech and printing

  • The owl praises your child using the text-to-speech engine built into the device’s operating system. The app passes it only the ready-made words of praise — no data about your child.
  • Printing sheets goes through the system print dialog (Android printing or AirPrint on iOS). The app does not send pictures anywhere by itself — they go only to the printer or PDF file you choose.

6. How long we keep data

  • Drawings, favourites and settings — until the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared. They disappear together with the app.
  • Statistical data — kept by Firebase Analytics according to the project settings, for a maximum of 14 months.

7. Your rights

You have the right to request access to your data, its rectification, erasure or restriction of processing, to object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority: the authority for your place of residence, or the Polish authority responsible for us, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, UODO), under Article 77 of the GDPR.

Because the app has no accounts, we cannot link statistical data to a specific person or device. The most effective way to remove data from the device is therefore to uninstall the app. Details are in the Deleting data section.

8. Changes to this policy

We will announce significant changes in the app’s update notes on Google Play and the App Store. The current version is always available at this address, with the date of the last change at the top of the page.

About the app

Two hundred pictures to colour — dinosaurs, bugs, flowers and animals. Your child paints with a finger at their own pace, and you can print favourite pages to colour with crayons.

Ages 3–8English, German, Polish No adsNo purchases No accountWorks offline

What’s inside

  • 200 coloring pages in four categories, 50 in each: dinosaurs, bugs, flowers and animals. New categories arrive in free updates.
  • One-tap fill — the colour fills an area right up to the outline.
  • Brush and eraser for fine touches, plus Undo and Redo.
  • Favourites — the heart remembers favourite pictures.
  • My drawings — a gallery of your child’s finished work.
  • Printable sheets — choose pictures, how many per page and whether to add captions, then print.

Parent zone

The Parent zone is protected by a PIN (0000 by default; you can change it). It contains the language setting, PIN change and the privacy policy.

Authors

The app is made by Sebastian Rosiński and Dawid Adamczyk. It is part of the Kidalu series, which also includes Reading by Syllables and Letters and Numbers.

Deleting data

The app has no user accounts, so there is no profile to close. Everything the app remembers is stored on the device.

Uninstalling the app deletes all data — your child’s drawings, favourites, settings and the Parent zone PIN. No copy remains on our side, because it never reached us.

How to clear data without uninstalling

Android:

  1. Open the Android Settings.
  2. Go to Apps and select Coloring.
  3. Open Storage, then choose Clear data.

You can also delete a single drawing in the app itself — in the My drawings gallery.

iPhone and iPad: iOS has no separate “clear app data” option — the only way is to delete the app:

  1. Open Settings → General → iPhone Storage (or iPad Storage).
  2. Select Coloring and tap Delete App.
  3. Or touch and hold the app icon on the Home Screen and choose Remove App.

Statistical data

Usage statistics are collected under an app instance identifier that does not point to any person. The identifier ceases to exist when the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared, and previously collected events are deleted automatically after a maximum of 14 months.

If you would like us to delete statistical data sooner, write to us — we will need the approximate time you used the app and the device model to find the right events.
