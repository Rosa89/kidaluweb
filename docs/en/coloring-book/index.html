Coloring Book

Two hundred pictures to colour: dinosaurs, bugs, flowers and animals. Your child fills areas with a single tap or paints with a brush, and you can print favourite pages on paper.

  • Kids aged 3–8
  • 200 free coloring pages
  • English, German, Polish
  • No ads, no account

Coming soon to Google Play

What's inside

  • Colour with one tap

    Tapping an area fills it right up to the outline. Thick, closed lines keep the paint from spilling outside the picture.

  • Brush and eraser

    A brush and an eraser handle the fine touches, and Undo and Redo fix any slip without starting over.

  • 200 pictures in four categories

    Dinosaurs, bugs, flowers and animals – 50 coloring pages each, all free. More categories arrive in free updates.

  • Printable sheets

    Pick pictures from any category, choose how many per page and whether to add captions, then print – for colouring with crayons, away from the screen.

  • Favourites and My drawings

    The heart remembers favourite pages, and finished work goes to the My drawings gallery.

  • Works offline

    Colouring needs no connection. Settings and external links wait in the PIN-protected parent area.

Who it's for

  • Kids aged 3–8 who love drawing and painting
  • Parents looking for calm, ad-free play
  • Preschools and after-school clubs – printable sheets for the whole group

Privacy

  • No ads and no promotional content aimed at children
  • The app needs no account or sign-in
  • Your child’s drawings stay on the device only

Compliant with COPPA, GDPR art. 8 and the Google Play Families policy, and, on iOS, Apple's App Store guidelines for apps in the Kids category.
