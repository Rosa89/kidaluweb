About the Reading by Syllables app

Reading by Syllables (Nauka czytania sylabami) is an educational app that helps children learn to read Polish by visually dividing text into syllables and highlighting them in alternating colours.

Features

  • Coloured syllables: automatic division of text into syllables with alternating colours
  • A library of publications: fairy tales, poems, stories and other educational texts
  • Personalisation: choice of syllable colours, font size and background themes
  • Your own text: type any text and see it in coloured syllables
  • Print and PDF: export material for printing
  • Content from Wolne Lektury: classics of Polish children's literature

Who is it for?

  • Children aged 4 to 8 learning to read Polish
  • Parents supporting reading practice at home
  • Teachers looking for classroom material

Deleting your account in the Reading by Syllables app

Every registered user can permanently delete their account and all related data at any time.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Nauka czytania sylabami app
  2. Sign in to your account
  3. Go to the Profile screen (the person icon on the bottom bar)
  4. Scroll down to the "Danger zone" section
  5. Tap the "Delete account" button
  6. Confirm the deletion by typing the word USUŃ (the Polish word for "delete")
  7. Tap "Confirm deletion"

What data will be deleted

Important information

  • Deleting the account is irreversible; neither the account nor the data can be restored
  • One-off (permanent) purchases will be lost
  • If you have an active subscription, it will be cancelled automatically
  • Data stored locally on the device (preferences) must be removed manually by uninstalling the app
