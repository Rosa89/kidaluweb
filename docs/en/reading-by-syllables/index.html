Reading by Syllables

The app splits every text into syllables and colours them alternately. Your child's eye stops getting lost mid-word, and reading turns from guessing into assembling.

  • Children aged 4–8
  • Polish only
  • No ads
  • No account, no sign-in

Get it on Google Play Read the documents

What's inside

  • Coloured syllables

    Text is split automatically by Polish syllable rules and neighbouring syllables get different colours. The child sees where one ends and the next begins.

  • A library of texts

    Fairy tales, poems and stories ready to read, including classics of Polish children's literature from Wolne Lektury.

  • Your own text

    Type anything: a postcard from grandma, a shopping list, a favourite song — and see it in coloured syllables.

  • Tuned to your child

    Set syllable colours, font size and background theme to whatever your child reads most comfortably.

  • Print and PDF

    Export any text and print it to practise away from the screen.

  • Reading progress

    The app remembers what your child has read and shows how far they got in each text.

Who it's for

  • Children aged 4–8 who are learning to read
  • Parents supporting reading at home
  • Teachers looking for classroom material

Privacy

  • No ads or promotional content aimed at children
  • We build no behavioural profiles and never link data between apps
  • The app has no accounts: no registration, no sign-in and no child profile

Compliant with COPPA, GDPR art. 8 and the Google Play Families policy, and, on iOS, Apple's App Store guidelines for apps in the Kids category.
